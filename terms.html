3. Free quota

  • The two-camera modes (picture-in-picture, top and bottom, left and right) have a monthly free quota: 30 photos and 10 videos, counted separately.
  • What counts against the quota is a finished photo or video that was successfully saved into Cam2gether's Library. Anything that was not saved successfully does not count.
  • The quota resets every month. The month starts when this iPhone first reads the quota record (usually the first time you open the app), and the quota then resets on the same day and time each month; in a shorter month that does not have that day, it resets on the last day of that month. The reset date and time differ from iPhone to iPhone, and the app shows when the next reset is (on the quota card at the top of Settings, and on the usage card you open from the ring in the camera's "..." tools bar). Unused quota does not carry over to the next month. The reset follows your iPhone's clock; Cam2gether has no server to check the time against.
  • When one kind of quota is used up and you have no subscription, you need a subscription to keep shooting that kind of content with both cameras until the next reset. Photos and videos are two separate quotas: using up one does not stop the other.
  • Normal (single camera) mode remains available and does not count against the quota. Opening the camera and the preview are never locked. Content you already shot is not deleted because of this.
  • Without a subscription, the results you save to Photos or share carry the watermark; see section 9.
  • The free quota cannot be transferred or credited against a subscription fee. Deleting a finished item does not give quota back.
  • The quota counter is kept on your iPhone and the developer cannot see it. After a reinstall, a new iPhone or a backup restore, the counter may be kept or may start over; the developer cannot guarantee either, and has no way to recover or reset your quota on a new iPhone. See section 6 of the Privacy Policy.
